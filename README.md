# Hover AI

Hover AI is an agentic desktop assistant built for creators, designers, and everyday users who think and speak in more than one language at a time.

---

## What it does

Most voice assistants were built for one accent, one language, one way of asking. Hover AI wasn't.

When you need help inside an app — finding a setting, figuring out a menu, navigating a workflow you've never touched — you trigger Hover AI with a keyboard shortcut and just talk. Not in a scripted command format. In whatever comes naturally: English, Yorùbá, Pidgin, Igbo, Hausa, or any mix of them mid-sentence.

Hover AI listens, reads your screen, tracks where your cursor is, and responds by placing a glowing beacon directly on the part of the interface you need to interact with. It doesn't just tell you what to do — it shows you, on the screen you're already looking at.

---

## The problem it solves

Voice interfaces have always assumed a very specific kind of user: someone who speaks standard American or British English, uses formal phrasing, and issues commands the way a command-line expects them.

That excludes most of the world.

African creators — building products in Lagos, shipping designs from Nairobi, coding in Accra — naturally code-switch. A sentence might start in English, lean on Yorùbá for nuance, and close in Pidgin for emphasis. No existing voice tool handles that gracefully. Most don't handle it at all.

Hover AI was built specifically for this reality. The speech model is trained on real code-switched speech, not textbook translations. The language profiles cover how people actually speak, not how formal language guides say they should.

---

## How it works

**Trigger.** A configurable keyboard shortcut activates Hover AI. There's no always-on microphone, no background listening. You decide when it starts.

**Speak.** You ask your question in whatever language feels right. Hover AI processes the audio ephemerally — it understands your intent, not just the words.

**Context.** At the same moment, Hover AI captures a frame of your current screen and notes where your cursor sits. It knows which app you're in, what panel is open, what state the UI is in.

**Guidance.** A glowing beacon appears on-screen, pointing to exactly the element you need — the right button, the right menu, the right field. If the task has multiple steps, it walks you through each one in sequence.

Nothing is recorded after the task is complete. Screen frames and audio are processed for the immediate query and discarded.

---

## Language support

Hover AI currently supports:

- **Nigeria + Pidgin** — the everyday connective tissue of West African English
- **Yorùbá** — including tonal patterns and common borrowings
- **Igbo** — including the directness of everyday Igbo speech
- **Hausa** — across its major regional variants
- **English** — including Nigerian, Ghanaian, and Kenyan English patterns

More language profiles ship every quarter, built from real recorded speech rather than synthetic training data.

---

## Who it's for

Hover AI is primarily for creators who use professional software daily — design tools like Figma, Photoshop, and Illustrator; developer environments like VS Code; productivity tools like Notion. If you've ever opened a new app and spent ten minutes hunting for a feature that should have taken thirty seconds, Hover AI is for you.

---

## Privacy

Hover AI is private by default. Screen captures and voice audio are processed only for the duration of a single query. Nothing is stored server-side, shared with third parties, or used for training without explicit opt-in. Microphone and screen permissions can be revoked or restricted from the settings panel at any time.
